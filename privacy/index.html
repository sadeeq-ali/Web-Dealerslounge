Privacy Policy

Last updated: 9 October 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how DealersLounge (“we,” “us,” or “our”) handles information when you use the DealersLounge mobile app, website, and related services. DealersLounge helps vehicle dealers manage inventory, post buyer demands, discover matches, and connect with other dealerships.

For privacy questions or requests, contact support@dealerslounge.pk.

1. Information we collect

The information we process depends on your role and the features you use.

  • Account and contact information: your name, phone number, email address where provided, account identifiers, authentication records, and account status. Phone verification uses one-time codes.
  • Identity and business verification: CNIC number, front and back CNIC images where submitted, business name, business type, business number, business card, and photos of your premises. We use these details to support dealership onboarding, identity review, and account approval.
  • Dealership profile: business contact details, logo, cover photo, premises photos, address, and business location.
  • Location: the address, city, area, country, and map coordinates you select for your business, inventory, or demands. With device permission, the app can obtain your current location to help select your business location. Location searches and selected places are processed through Google Maps and Places.
  • Listings and activity: vehicle details, photos, prices, buyer requirements, saved preferences, inventory and demand records, matches, dealership ratings and reviews, and related activity.
  • Team information: member names and phone numbers, invitations, roles, permissions, and dealership associations provided by you or your dealership administrator.
  • Device and service information: notification tokens, device platform, notification permission and preference settings, authentication identifiers, and service request and error records. Servers and service providers may process IP addresses and other connection information to deliver and secure the service.
  • Support communications: the contact information and content you provide when contacting us.

Please provide other people’s information only when you are authorized to do so. Do not include identity documents or unrelated personal information in vehicle listings or public dealership content.

2. How we use information

  • Create, authenticate, verify, and manage accounts and dealership profiles.
  • Store and display inventory and demands, identify relevant matches, and enable dealership contact.
  • Manage team access, invitations, permissions, preferences, and notifications.
  • Process dealership reviews and provide support.
  • Diagnose failures, maintain service security, prevent misuse, and enforce account access restrictions.
  • Process account recovery, deletion, and other privacy requests, and meet applicable legal obligations.

Notification preferences control optional app alerts. Authentication and account-related processing are needed to provide access to the service.

3. Sharing and service providers

Other dealers and your team. Dealership profiles, business contact details, locations, inventory, demands, and reviews are made available through the app’s dealership, listing, and matching features. Team access depends on assigned permissions. If you share a listing or match using your phone’s sharing features, the content goes to the recipients or services you choose. CNIC documents are collected for verification, not for publication as dealership listings.

Service providers. We use providers to operate the app and process information needed for their services:

  • Google Firebase: authentication, uploaded image storage, and push messaging, including account identifiers, uploaded files, and notification tokens as applicable. See Firebase Privacy and Security.
  • Google Maps and Places: map display, address search, place details, and location selection. See Google’s Privacy Policy.
  • SMS delivery providers: phone numbers and verification messages needed to deliver one-time codes. The service integrates with FastSMSAlerts for SMS delivery.
  • Hosting and infrastructure providers: database, server, network, and operational processing needed to store records and serve the app.
  • Apple and Google notification infrastructure: delivery of notifications to your device.

Legal and security needs. Information may be disclosed when necessary to comply with applicable law or valid legal process, investigate misuse, or protect the rights and safety of users and the service.

4. Permissions and your choices

  • Location: control device location permission in your phone settings. You can use the app’s location search and map selection to choose a business location.
  • Photos and camera: image selection or capture is used for documents, profiles, premises, and vehicles you choose to upload. Control available camera and photo permissions in your phone settings.
  • Notifications: change notification preferences in DealersLounge Settings or disable permission in your phone settings. A notification token and your permission status may be processed to manage delivery even when notification display is disabled.
  • Profile and content: use available editing and removal controls to update your details, inventory, and demands. Contact support when a field cannot be changed in the app.

Revoking a device permission prevents future access governed by that permission; it does not automatically remove information already submitted. Contact us or use account deletion to request removal.

5. Retention

We retain account, dealership, verification, and listing information while it is needed to operate your account and provide the service. The account-deletion process includes a 60-day recovery period, described below. Application log files are configured to rotate with a 14-day retention window; infrastructure providers may maintain separate operational records.

Deletion of active records does not necessarily remove independent copies you shared with other users, files still referenced by another record, or provider backups at the same instant. Limited information may need to remain for applicable legal obligations, security, or dispute resolution. Contact us for information about retention that applies to a particular record or request.

6. Account and data deletion

Request deletion in the app

Dealership owners can open Settings → Delete my account and confirm the request. You may be asked to sign in again for verification.

Request deletion without the app

Email support@dealerslounge.pk with the subject “DealersLounge account deletion request”. Include your registered phone number and dealership name so we can locate the account. You can also request deletion of specific personal information. Do not email your password, one-time code, or CNIC images. We may verify account ownership before acting.

When an owner’s account-deletion request is processed, the dealership and associated accounts and listings are made unavailable, and permanent deletion is scheduled after 60 days. The owner can sign in and follow the restoration flow before the stated deadline to cancel deletion.

After the recovery period, the deletion process removes or anonymizes associated personal and business information, including names, phone numbers, email addresses, CNIC numbers, dealership details, inventory, demands, profile media records, settings, and related records. Authentication and unreferenced stored media are queued for cleanup. Non-identifying account records may remain to prevent old credentials from restoring access. Shared media still referenced elsewhere is not removed by that cleanup.

Deleting a dealership affects its team members. Team members who cannot access the owner-only deletion control can contact support to request removal of their own personal information. Uninstalling the app does not delete your account.

7. Security and processing locations

The service uses authenticated access and role-based permissions to restrict account operations. We take measures to protect information, but no system or transmission method can guarantee absolute security. Keep your verification codes private and report suspected unauthorized access to support.

Our infrastructure and service providers may process information in countries other than where you live. The applicable privacy laws may differ. Contact us if you need information about where your data is processed.

8. Your privacy requests

Depending on the law that applies to you, you may have rights to access, correct, delete, or obtain a copy of your information, or to object to or restrict certain processing. You can withdraw device permissions using your phone settings. Send other requests to support@dealerslounge.pk.

We may ask for information necessary to verify your identity and understand your request. Where applicable, you may also raise a concern with your local data protection authority.

9. Children’s privacy

DealersLounge is a business service intended for vehicle dealers and dealership teams, not a service directed to children. If you believe a child has submitted personal information, contact us so we can investigate and address the information.

10. Website and external links

This website does not include advertising trackers, analytics scripts, or signup forms. Its fonts and images are served with the site. The hosting service may process connection information to deliver pages and maintain security. Email links open your email application and send information only when you choose to send a message.

External websites and services have their own privacy practices. Review their policies when following links or sharing information with them.

11. Policy changes and contact

We may update this policy as the service or its data practices change. Updates will be posted on this page with a revised date. Where required, we will provide additional notice or obtain consent.
