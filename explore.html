Who is DealersLounge for?

DealersLounge is built for vehicle dealers and dealership teams who want to manage stock, share buyer requirements, and discover opportunities with other dealers.

What is a demand?

A demand is a vehicle requirement from a buyer. You post what you’re looking for so it can be matched with relevant inventory.

How do matches work?

DealersLounge connects vehicle inventory with relevant demands. You can review a match and contact the dealership to discuss the opportunity.
