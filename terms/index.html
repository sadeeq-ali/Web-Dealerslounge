Accounts and business information

Provide accurate information, use an account you are authorized to operate and keep verification codes private. Dealership owners are responsible for assigning appropriate team permissions. Do not impersonate another person or dealership, use someone else's identity documents, or attempt to bypass access restrictions.

Listings, demands and reviews

Post only content you own or have permission to use. Vehicle details, prices, availability and reviews must be accurate to the best of your knowledge. Update or remove outdated listings. Reviews must describe your own relevant experience; do not post fabricated reviews, manipulate ratings or demand payment to remove a review.

You retain ownership of your content. You permit DealersLounge to store, process and display it to operate the service, including matching, sharing features you use and content moderation. Our Privacy Policy explains how personal information is handled.

Prohibited content and conduct

  • Illegal, fraudulent, stolen or misleading offers; scams; impersonation; and deceptive pricing or vehicle information.
  • Sexually explicit content, child sexual abuse material, exploitation, graphic violence, threats, harassment, hateful or discriminatory content.
  • Posting another person's CNIC, identity documents, private contact information or other personal data without appropriate authorization. Identity documents belong only in the designated verification flow.
  • Copyright or trademark infringement, spam, malicious links, harmful software, unauthorized scraping and attempts to interfere with the service or access another account.
  • Abusing reports, evading account restrictions or using another account to continue prohibited conduct.

Content concerns and enforcement

Report content or conduct concerns to support@dealerslounge.pk, identifying the listing, review or dealership and explaining the concern. Do not send passwords, verification codes or CNIC images. For immediate physical danger, contact local emergency services.

We may investigate reports, remove or restrict content, limit matching or contact access, and suspend or close accounts that violate these rules. Contact support to ask about or appeal a decision. A report does not guarantee that content will be removed.

Confirm vehicle documentation, condition, price and transaction terms independently. Buyers and sellers are responsible for their own agreements and applicable obligations. Do not send payment solely because a listing appears in the app.
